## Editorial policy

- Cite specific mission pages rather than just NASA's homepage.
- Dates describe **launch**, **last signal**, or **official mission close** as distinct events.
- Do not equate “not operating” with “abandoned intact.”
- Avoid claims of extraterrestrial life: water indicators and potentially habitable environments are *not* proof of life.
- Never claim a real-time NASA integration unless code actually provides one.
- The quiz choices are educational paraphrases, not statements quoted from NASA.

## License and imagery

NASA's public information being accessible does not automatically mean every image, logo, video, or third-party element is unrestricted. Cite specific image sources and check NASA's media guidelines and other rights before adding external visuals. This starter contains no NASA photo downloads.
