## Ethical production checklist

- Clearly label AI-generated footage and distinguish it from original NASA/stock footage where submission rules require it.
- Attribute visual assets to creators and verify their usage rights.
- Do not invent student testing or quote fictional metrics as observed results.
- Verify every scientific number shown in a screenshot before narrating it.
- Keep timing consistent with the final competition's published requirements.
